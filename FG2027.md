# FG2027 — design decisions

The study tests landmark groups and fusion, using a shared training recipe across
models. It does not compare each architecture at its individually tuned optimum.
Exact settings and combinations live in [the config](configs/fg2027.json) and
[experiment matrix](experiments/matrix.csv).

- **Inputs:** ViTPose only. The pose subset follows the earlier study for continuity;
  it includes head points, so adding face is not the first introduction of head information.
- **Positional encoding:** omitted based on the earlier IberSPEECH experiments and
  Luna-Jiménez et al. (2025). Stored frame order alone does not make the encoder-only
  model order-sensitive: without positional encoding, its mean-pooled output is
  invariant to frame permutations.
- **Training:** retain the earlier recipe, including a constant learning rate.
  No scheduler or warmup was specified in the paper. SPOTER is an adapted baseline,
  not an exact reproduction.
- **Fair inputs:** every condition uses the same clips, splits and frames.
  Person selection uses pose even for hands-only runs; the classifier still receives
  only the requested landmark groups.
- **Late fusion:** modality encoders are trained jointly from scratch, rather than
  loaded from the early-fusion runs. Extra encoders increase capacity, so any gains
  cannot be attributed to fusion alone.
- **Scope:** one seed for the completed batch. Additional seeds and paired bootstrap
  analysis of saved test predictions are deferred.
